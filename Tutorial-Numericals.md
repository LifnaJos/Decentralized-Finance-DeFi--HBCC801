## Tutorial Questions
### Q1. A person wants to send $1,000 from the USA to Australia through a traditional bank. The following fees are charged (a) Exchange rate markup: $40 (b) Outbound wire fee: $65 (c) Inbound wire fee: $45
- a. Calculate the total transaction fee charged by the bank.
- b. Determine the amount received in Australia after deducting fees.
- c. If the settlement takes 3 business days, what is the average fee per day of waiting?

### Q2. A user sends $1,000 internationally using

**Scenario A** - Centralized Finance (CeFi) through banks with the following charges (a) Exchange rate markup: $30 (b) Outbound wire fee: $25 (c) Inbound wire fee: $15 (d) Settlement time: 3 days

**Scenario B** – Decentralized Finance (DeFi) using crypto with the following charges (a) Network gas fee: $3 (b) Settlement time: 2 minutes
- a. Calculate the total fees in both scenarios.
- b. Calculate the amount received by the recipient in each case.
- c. Determine the cost savings when using DeFi instead of CeFi.

### Q3. A user sends $1,000 internationally. 
(a) Bank-based transfer (CeFi): Total fees: $70
(b) Crypto-based transfer (DeFi): Network fee: $4
- a. Calculate the percentage fee charged by CeFi.
- b. Calculate the percentage fee charged by DeFi.
- c. How much percentage cost reduction does DeFi provide?

### Q4. Two users send $1,000 internationally.
|User#| Method |Total Fee| Settlement Time |
| :-----: | :--: | :------: :--------:|
|User 1 | Bank Transfer (CeFi) | $70	| 4 days |

- a. Calculate the difference in transaction cost.
- b. Convert 4 days into minutes and compare the settlement time.
- c. Calculate how many times faster DeFi settlement is compared to CeFi.

### Q5. A company sends $10,000 internationally.
**CeFi fees (same structure):** Exchange rate markup: 3%; Outbound wire fee: $25; Inbound wire fee: $15
**DeFi fees:** Network fee: $5
- a. Calculate the total cost of sending money via CeFi.
- b. Calculate the total cost via DeFi.
